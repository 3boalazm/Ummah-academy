What You'll Learn

Memorization that doesn't stick isn't really memorization — it's temporary recall. This programme is built around retention, not just addition: what you've already memorized gets revisited and reinforced on a deliberate schedule, so new verses build on a genuinely solid foundation rather than a shaky one.

Programs are tailored by age group, since a 7-year-old and a 35-year-old need very different pacing, session structure, and encouragement to sustain the habit long-term.

  • Structured revision, not just new memorization piled on top
  • A program shaped to your specific age group
  • An open-ended programme — progress at a sustainable pace, not a rushed deadline
