What You'll Learn

Reading Arabic and speaking it comfortably are two different skills — plenty of students can sound out a text but freeze in an actual conversation. This course is built entirely around speaking practice: real back-and-forth conversation with a live teacher, correcting pronunciation and phrasing in the moment.

A natural companion to Noor Al-Bayan or Grammar, for students who want the spoken side of the language developing alongside the written.

  • Live conversation practice, not passive vocabulary drilling
  • Real-time correction of pronunciation and phrasing
  • Confidence speaking day-to-day, not just reading silently
