What You'll Learn

Nahw (syntax) and Sarf (morphology) are what let a student move from recognizing words to genuinely understanding sentence structure and meaning on their own — reading a text directly rather than always relying on someone else's translation.

This is a more analytical course than Noor Al-Bayan's reading foundations, best suited to students who can already read Arabic script comfortably and want to understand *why* the language works the way it does.

  • Sentence structure (Nahw) and word formation (Sarf)
  • Direct access to classical and Qur'anic texts, not translation-dependent
  • Builds directly on Noor Al-Bayan reading fluency
