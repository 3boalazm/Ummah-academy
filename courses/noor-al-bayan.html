What You'll Learn

Everything else — Tajweed, Hifz, Tafseer — depends on this first step being genuinely solid: reading Arabic script itself, without hesitation or guessing. This course starts from the absolute beginning, connecting letters and short vowels, and builds methodically toward confident, independent reading straight from the Mushaf.

No prior Arabic knowledge assumed at all — this is specifically designed for students starting from zero.

  • Starts from zero — no prior Arabic reading required
  • Built on Noor Al-Bayan and Al-Qaida Al-Nooraniyyah
  • The direct foundation for Tajweed and Hifz that follow
