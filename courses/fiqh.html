What You'll Learn

Most Muslims pray and perform Wudu the way they were shown once, years ago — without ever having it actually checked. This course walks through the practical rulings of daily worship properly, correcting the small, common errors that quietly persist for years once they've become habit.

Because sessions are 1-on-1 and live, your teacher can watch and correct the specific way *you* perform an action — not just describe the rule in the abstract.
