What You'll Learn

Tajweed is a skill, not a fact you memorize once — it has to be heard and corrected, over and over, until it's automatic. This course moves through three clearly defined levels, each with its own measurable milestones, so you always know exactly where you stand.

The curriculum draws on the same proven, centuries-tested texts Al-Azhar-trained teachers everywhere rely on — nothing improvised, nothing invented for the sake of an app.
