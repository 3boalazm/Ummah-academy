What You'll Learn

This course exists for one purpose: correct, confident recitation. Every session is live and 1-on-1, so your teacher hears exactly where a letter, a stop, or a stretch needs correcting — in real time, not after the fact on a recording.

You'll work directly with a chain of transmission that traces back through your teacher's own teachers to the Prophet ﷺ himself — the same standard of accuracy Al-Azhar has maintained for a thousand years.

  • Real-time correction on every session, not delayed feedback
  • A verified chain of transmission (Sanad), not self-taught guesswork
  • Paced to your current level — complete beginners welcome
