01 Foundations

Brand is a promise, not a decoration.

Ummah Academy sells trust: a chain of scholarship a parent can verify. The identity stays quiet so the proof can be loud.

The idea

Calm confidence

Sakinah — the stillness that comes with certainty. We design like a well-kept study room: warm light, clear surfaces, nothing shouting.

We are not

A hype machine

No countdown timers, no “last chance”, no gold gradients on everything. Urgency is the opposite of trust in religious education.

The promise

Traceable scholarship

Ijazah, Sanad, vetting steps and named teachers — surfaced in the interface, not buried in an About page.

Ink · #281547 on light
White · on night panels
Clear space = ½ height

Logo rules

  • — The calligraphic أمة inside an arch is the identity. No typeset wordmark sits beside it.
  • — Minimum height 22px; never recolor outside ink or white; never outline, rotate or add shadow.
  • — Two colourways ship as SVG: logo-ink.svg and logo-white.svg.
  • — In RTL the mark keeps its position in the flow; it is a symbol, not a directional glyph.

03 Foundations

Geist for clarity. Instrument for warmth. Amiri for the Qur’an.

Four families, each with one job. Arabic is not a translation layer — it is a first-class script with its own line heights and rhythm.

--font-sans · GeistUI · headings · body

Learn the Qur’an with confidence.

Variable 100–900. The default for everything except quoted verse and editorial accents. Tight tracking on display sizes (−0.04em), normal below 20px.

--font-serif · Instrument SerifAccent only

One step at a time.

Italic only, one accent phrase per heading, never for body copy or buttons. It supplies the human hand that Geist’s geometry lacks.

--font-arabic · IBM Plex Sans ArabicAR UI

تعلّم القرآن بثقة، خطوة بخطوة.

Line-height 1.9 for Arabic UI (vs 1.6 for Latin). No letter-spacing — it breaks the joins. Left-aligned numerals stay Latin inside Arabic text.

--font-quran · AmiriQur’anic text

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

Every verse, basmala and du‘a’ is set in Amiri at 2.0 line-height, never in the UI sans. Tajweed colouring is a separate, opt-in layer.

ﷺ — the honorific is rendered by the same font through a unicode-range rule.

Typesetting rules

  • — Measure: 62–72 characters. Never full-width body text on desktop.
  • — Display line-height 0.94–1.06; body 1.6; Arabic UI 1.9.
  • — One accent phrase per section heading, in violet or Instrument italic — not both.
  • — Prices use tabular numerals: font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums.
  • — Qur’anic text always has a lang="ar" ancestor and Amiri; never justify it.

05 Foundations

Glass is a layer system, not a filter.

Five layers make a Sakinah surface: a gradient fill, backdrop blur with saturation, a 1px rim light, a specular sheen and a two-part shadow. Remove any one and it collapses into grey plastic.

Gradient fill. White 80% → 44% at 155°. Direction matters: light comes from top-left.
Backdrop blur 22px + saturation 1.2. The aurora must still be felt through the panel.
Rim light. A 1px gradient border that is brighter at the top edge.
Specular sheen. A soft white streak at 18% opacity, offset to the top-right.
Shadow. Two parts: a tight contact shadow and a wide ambient one. Never a single blurred drop.

06 Foundations

Motion with a heartbeat, never a jolt.

One easing curve for entrances, one spring for direct manipulation, one long drift for atmosphere. If an animation does not inform or reassure, it is removed.

Principle 01

Calm, not slow

Entrances are 500–900ms but never block input. A button responds in 150ms even if its panel is still settling.

Principle 02

Teach the hierarchy

Stagger follows reading order. No random delays, no looped attention-seekers near the CTA.

Principle 03

Respect the body

prefers-reduced-motion removes drift, reveals and floats. prefers-reduced-transparency makes glass solid.

10 Craft

Accessibility is a design constraint, not a checklist.

AA is the floor. Text on glass targets 7:1; secondary ink only appears on ≥56% white glass. Every interactive element keeps the same violet focus ring, in both themes.

Live contrast checker

WCAG 2.2
Your teacher hears every letter.

Non-negotiables

  • Focus. 2.5px violet outline, 3px offset, never removed. Tiles show it on the visual box, not the hidden input.
  • Targets. 44×44px minimum; 8px between adjacent actions. Sticky CTA respects safe-area insets.
  • Text on glass. Ink ≥ 7:1. Secondary ink only on ≥ 56% white fill. Never light grey on a gradient.
  • Motion. prefers-reduced-motion kills aurora drift, reveals, floats. prefers-reduced-transparency solidifies glass.
  • Forced colours. Glass, buttons and tiles keep 1px borders so they survive Windows High Contrast.
  • Semantics. One H1 per page, heading order intact, landmarks named, errors linked with aria-describedby.

11 Craft

Words are part of the surface.

Calm, specific, never salesy. We name the mechanism — Ijazah, Sanad, 30-minute sessions — instead of stacking adjectives. Every string exists in five languages before it ships.

Voice 01

Specific over superlative

“A four-stage check” beats “the best teachers”. Numbers, names and steps earn trust.

Voice 02

Reassure, don’t pressure

No countdowns, no scarcity. “Two free lessons. No card.” is the strongest CTA in the product.

Voice 03

Respect the reader

Second person, plain sentences, 8th-grade reading level. Arabic is written natively, not translated word-for-word.

Writing rules

  • — Sentence case for headings, buttons and labels. No Title Case, no ALL CAPS sentences.
  • — “Qur’an” with the apostrophe; “the Prophet ﷺ” with the honorific, never “(PBUH)”.
  • — Prices: $59 / month. Never “only $59!!”.
  • — Dates localise through Intl; never assemble a date string by hand.
  • — No emoji in product UI. One exception: WhatsApp message templates.
Do / don’t
✓ Do

✗ Don’t

“Unlock your child’s FULL potential today — limited seats!”

12 Engineering

Tokens are the contract.

One CSS file, seven cascade layers, no preprocessor. Components consume semantic tokens — never raw hex — so a theme change is a token change.
