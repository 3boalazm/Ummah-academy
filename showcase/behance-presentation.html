01 Snapshot

A quiet product for a serious decision.

A parent choosing a Qur’an teacher is not shopping. They are handing over something they love. Every screen had to earn that, without ever raising its voice.

Client Ummah Academy

Live 1-on-1 Qur’an, Tajweed, Arabic & Islamic studies with Al-Azhar scholars. Replacing a WordPress site with a native one.

What shipped Sakinah Glass + 195 pages

A hand-written design system, 23 routes in five languages, an installable PWA, and a separate operations portal.

Users Parents & adults

Guardians 30–45 in the Gulf, Europe and North America; adult returners; teachers; an ops team.

Constraint No runtime framework

Static Astro output. ~2 KB of JS per page. Self-hosted fonts. Inlined icons.

Team 1 designer + 2 engineers

Design and design-engineering in one pair of hands; content and academic review in-house.

The win Trust you can point at

Ijazah, Sanad and the four-stage vetting process are interface elements — not paragraphs on an About page.

Client-reported figures 50+ countries · 1,000+ students

95% continue after month one; 12+ Ijazahs granted. Supplied by the academy, not independently audited.

02 Context

The brief was a rebuild. The job was a reassurance.

The academy already had demand. What it did not have was a place where a careful parent could see, in under a minute, who would be teaching their child and why that person could be trusted.

“I don’t need the cheapest. I need to know who is teaching my daughter.”

SAParent interview · London
What the audit found
  1. Trust was claimed, not shown.

    Every peer page used the word “certified”. None showed an Ijazah, a chain of transmission, or how a teacher is vetted. The strongest asset was invisible.

  2. The path to a first lesson was an obstacle course.

    Enquiry → email → WhatsApp → time zones → payment. Each step lost a family that had already decided to start.

  3. Five languages in the footer, one language in the experience.

    Arabic existed, but as a mirrored English page. Line heights, numerals and honorifics were wrong for the script.

  4. The brand was louder than the scholarship.

    Gold gradients and urgency banners signalled a discount shop, not a place of learning.

7peer academies reviewed
5languages required
14weeks, brief to launch
The non-negotiables

Keep every SEO URL.The rebuild could not break the 23 routes that already ranked.

Ship the system, not the page.A new block must be assemblable from the system in an afternoon.

04 Strategy

Four principles, chosen to be uncomfortable.

Principles only matter when they cost something. Each of these removed a tempting option from the table.

01

Proof over persuasion

Ijazah, Sanad and vetting get first-class UI. Testimonials come after the mechanism, never instead of it.

02

Calm, not hype

Glass over dawn instead of neon urgency. The interface lowers the heart rate; it never spikes it.

03

One decision per screen

The router asks “who is learning?” before anything else. The wizard asks three questions, not nine.

04

Traceable claims

Every number has a footnote. Every testimonial has consent. No “world-class” without a definition.

06 System

A system you can feel through the glass.

Sixty-plus tokens, 34 components, seven cascade layers, one CSS file. The rule that made it coherent: no component may invent a colour, a radius or a shadow.

Tokens
--aubergine-800: #2e1d46; --violet-600: #6b3fa0; --gold-400: #d9b26a; --ink: #1e1530; --accent: var(--violet-600); --r-lg: 28px; --blur: 22px; --ease-out: cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1);

Every semantic token has a dark twin. A theme is a token swap, not a stylesheet.

Radii & rhythm
10142028pill

Controls are pills or 14px. Cards are 20–28. Sections are 40. Gutter 18 → 40; section rhythm 72 → 136.

Motion

08 Mobile

The lesson happens on a phone.

Most families meet us on a mid-range Android in a kitchen. The mobile experience is not a responsive afterthought: it is the product, with 44px targets, a bottom dock and a classroom built for one hand.

Thumb first

Primary actions sit in the lower third. The sticky CTA respects env(safe-area-inset-bottom) and hides on scroll-up.

Installable

Manifest, maskable icons and a service worker. Offline, the last pages visited still open — including the lesson brief.

Portal, separately

The operations portal is a separate Node process: students, families, session ledger, credits, permissions. Not part of the static build.

10 Motion

Movement that lowers the pulse.

One curve for entrances, one spring for direct manipulation, one slow drift for the sky. Nothing loops near a call to action; nothing animates a layout property.

Motion rules
  • — Entrances never block input; a click responds in 150ms even while a panel settles.
  • — Stagger follows reading order, 60ms apart, and stops after six items.
  • — The aurora drifts with transform on a fixed layer — zero layout cost.
  • — prefers-reduced-motion removes drift, reveals and floats; nothing becomes unreachable.
  • — Hover effects exist only on (hover: hover) devices.

12 Craft

Fast is a design decision.

A parent on 3G in Cairo should get the proof as quickly as a parent on fibre in Toronto. So the stack stayed boring: static HTML, one CSS file, a few kilobytes of JavaScript.

195pages built5 locales · 23 routes
~2 KBJS per pagegzipped · no framework
34componentsone design system
0image requests for coversgenerated CSS / SVG
What that looks like in code
@layer tokens, base, layout, glass, components, motion, utilities; .glass { background: var(--glass-fill); backdrop-filter: blur(var(--blur)) saturate(1.2); box-shadow: var(--shadow-glass); border-radius: var(--r-lg); } .glass::before { /* 1px rim light */ } .glass::after { /* specular sheen */ }

Seven cascade layers mean a component style can never accidentally beat a token. Fonts are self-hosted; icons are inlined at build time.

What we deliberately didn’t ship
  1. A client-side router

    The site is static and the navigation is links. Nothing to hydrate, nothing to break offline.

  2. An animation library

    Three keyframes and an IntersectionObserver were enough for the entire product.

  3. A component framework

    Astro components render to HTML; the design system is CSS, so it outlives any framework.

  4. A portrait library

    Monograms age better than stock photos and avoid consent problems with children.

“Calm is not the absence of design. It is design that has stopped competing with the content.”

Lesson 01

Name the mechanism

“Ijazah”, “Sanad”, “30 minutes” persuade more than any adjective. Specificity is the brand.

Lesson 02

Design the proof, not the page

Turning the chain into a reusable component made it appear on six pages instead of one.

Lesson 03

RTL is a system test

Arabic exposed every hard-coded margin and every icon that assumed a direction. It made the English better too.
