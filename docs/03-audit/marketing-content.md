# Audit · Marketing & content

## 1. Message hierarchy (homepage, v1)

Claim frequency counted on the v1 homepage:

| Claim | Times stated |
|---|---|
| Al-Azhar / Azhari | 9 |
| 1-on-1 / live | 8 |
| Flexible / 24-7 / timezones | 6 |
| Certificates / Ijazah | 7 |
| Female teachers / safe | 4 |
| 50+ countries | 4 |

Repetition without new evidence reads as filler. The v2 rule is **one claim per section, each paired with proof** (a mechanism, a number, a quote or a visual).

## 2. Copy issues

| Issue | Example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Generic headlines | "Numbers That Reflect Real Quality", "4 Comprehensive Paths to Islamic Knowledge" | Specific, benefit-led: "Four ways in — pick where you are today" |
| Hype superlatives in our own voice | "every obstacle removed", "Simply extraordinary" (the latter is a testimonial, so it is kept) | Calm, factual tone. Let customers supply the superlatives. |
| Inline bilingual duplication | "Why Ummah Academy — لماذا أكاديمية الأمة" on every eyebrow | English pages in English; Arabic pages in Arabic |
| Mixed Egyptian colloquial and MSA in Arabic | "مش متأكد من مستواك؟ خد اختبار…" | Warm Modern Standard Arabic throughout `/ar/` |
| Bolt-on SEO paragraphs | "Welcome — Quran & Arabic Classes Online…" at the bottom of each page | Keywords woven into the hero subcopy and section intros |
| Inconsistent promises | "5 minutes" / "2 hours" / "within a day" | One promise: "We reply on WhatsApp, usually within minutes and always within 2 hours." Business to confirm the SLA. |

## 3. CTA strategy

- **Primary:** *Book 2 free sessions* → `/contact/` booking wizard (prefilled by context: course, teacher, plan, placement result).
- **Secondary:** *Find my level* → placement test, for the undecided.
- **Tertiary:** *WhatsApp*, a persistent, low-commitment channel, which matters greatly for this audience.
- **Removed:** "Explore Courses" as a hero CTA (navigation does that job) and duplicated end-of-page CTAs stacked on top of each other.

## 5. Bilingual strategy

- The **English** site stays primary (search demand comes from English-speaking diaspora markets).
- The **Arabic** site serves Arabic-speaking families abroad (Gulf expats, Arab diaspora in Europe and North America) who want their children taught by Azhari teachers. It covers the core funnel pages: home, courses, tracks, pricing, teachers, about, booking, placement and FAQ.
- The blog stays in English for now. Arabic articles are a content-roadmap item.
- `hreflang` pairs and an explicit language switch are added; the Arabic URLs are clean (`/ar/pricing/`).

## 6. Objections the FAQ must answer (ranked)

1. Is the trial really free, and what happens after it? *(the "after" part was missing on v1)*
2. How much does it cost, and can I cancel or pause?
3. Can my daughter or I have a female teacher?
4. My child is 5 or 6 — is that too young?
5. I don't speak Arabic — will I understand?
6. Will the times work in my time zone?
7. Are sessions recorded, and can I see progress?
8. Who exactly are the teachers?
