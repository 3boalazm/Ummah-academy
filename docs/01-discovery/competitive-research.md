## 2. What this means for Ummah Academy

### Table stakes — they do not differentiate

Nearly every competitor claims **"Al-Azhar certified", "1-on-1 live", "free trial", "female teachers", "24/7 / all time zones"** and **"kids & adults"**. Leading with these, as the live site does, makes Ummah Academy sound like everyone else.

### Real differentiators Ummah Academy can own

1. **Verified Ijazah and Sanad, plus a published four-stage vetting process.** Competitors assert certification; Ummah can *show* the chain and the process. This is the strongest trust story.
2. **The Itqan thematic method,** a named, explainable pedagogy with a blog article behind it.
3. **Transparent, lower prices at higher frequency.**
   - Ummah's 30-minute plans: 4×/week **$59** vs $80 at Super Muslim and Tarteel (**about 26% cheaper**); 5×/week **$74** vs $100 (**26% cheaper**).
   - Al-Azhar quality at fair prices is a defensible message.
   - Note that 60-minute plans compare less favourably per minute. Pricing architecture is analysed in `03-audit/business.md`.
4. **The placement test and the Family Portal.** Product experiences most competitor sites lack.
5. **A modern, calm, trustworthy experience.** The category is dominated by dated templates (see the screenshots of the competitor sites above). A studio-grade site is itself a differentiator.

### Offer gaps relative to competitors (business decisions, not built)

- No **money-back guarantee** (Super Muslim offers 30 days).
- No **sibling or family discount** published (Super Muslim: 10%). This is a natural fit given the "Family Plan" testimonial and the Family Portal.
- No lead magnet beyond the placement test; Tasleem uses tools such as prayer times and an AI recitation check.

These are logged as backlog items for the business. None of them were invented on the site.

## 3. UX patterns observed (what good looks like)

- **Price clarity next to frequency** (Super Muslim shows "2 classes/week · 30 min · $40"). Ummah's pricing page does this well; the homepage does not.
- **One primary CTA** ("Book your free trial") repeated consistently, with WhatsApp as the secondary option.
- **Process transparency** ("advisor contacts you on WhatsApp within 24h → matched teacher → 30-min trial") reduces anxiety before submitting.
