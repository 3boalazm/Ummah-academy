Our vision is to create a comprehensive, integrated program that motivates Muslims worldwide to return to the Qur'an — not merely for display recitation, but for genuine mastery (Itqan), deep contemplation (Tadabbur), and a heartfelt connection to Allah's words.

Our Journey

How Ummah Academy Began

  1. 2022 — The Seed

    A Problem We Couldn't Ignore

    Our founder noticed Muslim families in the West struggled to find qualified, authentic Qur'an teachers — relying on uncertified volunteers or expensive local schools with inflexible schedules.

  2. 2023 — The Solution

    Building the Al-Azhar Connection

    We began recruiting directly from Al-Azhar University — focusing on scholars holding Ijazah certificates with documented chains to the Prophet ﷺ.

  3. 2024 — Launch

    Ummah Academy Goes Live

    We officially launched with 20 teachers and 150 students across 12 countries. Our first Ijazah was granted to a student in the UK within our first year.

  4. 2025 — Growth

    Expanding the Family

    Expanded to 100+ scholars, 1,000+ students, 50+ countries, and introduced the Ummah Cubs program for children age 5+.

  5. 2026 — Today

    A Platform for the Ummah

    Today we deliver 400+ live teaching hours monthly, with 12+ Ijazah certificates issued, 95% student retention, and a complete Family Portal.

Our Teaching Philosophy

The Itqan Methodology — Perfection Over Display

Most platforms teach memorization as performance. We fundamentally reject this. Our "Itqan" (perfection) method insists on mastery, precision, and Tadabbur at every stage.

Thematic Verse Grouping

We group verses by theme and meaning — allowing students to understand context.

Real-Time Makhraj Correction

Live 1-on-1 sessions let teachers instantly correct articulation points.

Progressive Level System

Three clearly defined levels with measurable milestones per track.

Chain of Transmission (Sanad)

Ijazah graduates receive a documented chain tracing back to the Prophet ﷺ.
