Getting Started

How quickly will you respond?

We reply via WhatsApp within 2 hours — usually much faster, across all timezones.

Are the 2 free trial sessions really free?

Yes — completely free, no credit card, no commitment.

Can I request a specific teacher?

Yes — mention their name when you book. We'll do our best to match you.

Courses & Methodology

What if I don't speak Arabic?

Many of our students start with zero Arabic. Every teacher is fully bilingual — Arabic and English.

What makes your teaching approach different?

Our Itqan Methodology focuses on mastery and Tadabbur (contemplation), not just recitation — thematic verse grouping and real-time correction, rather than rote memorization.

Do I get a certificate or Ijazah?

Yes. Every student receives a completion certificate per level. Students pursuing recitation/Hifz tracks can work toward a formal Ijazah, with a documented chain of transmission back to the Prophet ﷺ.

Teachers

Who are your teachers?

All Al-Azhar University graduates holding Ijazah certificates, experienced in teaching both Arabic and non-Arabic speakers.

Can I request a female teacher for my daughter or wife?

Yes. We have dedicated female teachers for women and children, in a fully private, faith-conscious setting.

Scheduling & Technology

What platform do sessions run on?

Zoom or Google Meet, with interactive whiteboard tools for marking up Tajweed.

What if I need to miss a session?

Let us know with as much notice as you can — our packages include a flexible rescheduling policy (24-hour notice) so a missed session isn't a lost one.

Do you cover my timezone?

Yes — sessions run around the clock, so you book whatever time genuinely fits your schedule, not ours.

Family & Kids

My child is 5 — is that too young?

Not at all — Ummah Cubs accepts children from age 5, with short, gamified 30-minute sessions.

Can I track my child's progress?

Yes, through the Family Portal — one account covering live progress tracking, session recordings, and WhatsApp notifications for every linked child.

Pricing & Payment

How much does it cost?

Packages range from $40 to $128 per month depending on track and session frequency — every plan starts with 2 free trial sessions first.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa, MasterCard, PayPal, and Western Union / MoneyGram.
