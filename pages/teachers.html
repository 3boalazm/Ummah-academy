What Sets Them Apart

Not just teachers. Verified scholars.

Anyone can teach a Zoom call. Few can trace their knowledge back through an unbroken chain to the Prophet ﷺ himself.

Chain of Transmission

A verified Sanad, not just a certificate

Every teacher holds an Ijazah — formal authorization to teach Qur'anic recitation, carrying a documented chain of transmission back through their own teachers. It's the same standard of scholarly rigor Al-Azhar has upheld for a thousand years, brought to a laptop screen.

Al-Azhar University Trained

Graduates of Al-Azhar's Faculties of Sharia, Arabic Language, and Islamic Studies — the oldest continuously operating seat of Islamic scholarship in the world.
